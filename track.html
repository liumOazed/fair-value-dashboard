How the forward test works

  • Each month the dashboard records its signals the day they are made, with that day's prices: the Monthly Top 5 picks, the Growth Screen top 50, the 5 resilient compounders, and the full-model stocks below fair value and below their buy-below price.
  • A recorded group is never edited afterwards and nothing is filled in with hindsight, so there is no look-ahead or survivorship bias. A stock that stops trading keeps its last known price.
  • Each group is compared with buying SPY on the same day. "Beat SPY" is the share of the group's stocks that did better than SPY.
  • Too early to judge. Weeks or months of returns are mostly noise. A fair verdict needs at least 12 months and several monthly groups.
