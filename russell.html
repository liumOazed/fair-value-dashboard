How the Russell 2000 screen works

1. Members

  • The iShares Russell 2000 ETF (IWM) holdings file in data/universe/IWM_holdings.csv, uploaded by hand (iShares does not serve it to GitHub). Russell rebuilds the index each June and, from 2026, adds quarterly updates, so a new upload each quarter is enough.

2. Quality gate (about 40% of the index loses money)

  • Market value at least $1B and at least $5M traded a day.
  • At least 4 fiscal years of statements.
  • An operating profit, and free cash flow positive in at least 3 of the last 4 years.
  • Banks and insurers: a net profit and ROE of at least 8% instead (their free cash flow means little).

3. Score

  • The same Growth Quality Score as the Growth Screen (quality, growth, value, financial safety), but every percentile is taken among the small caps that passed the gate, never against the S&P 500.

4. Quick fair value for the top 50

  • The Monthly Top 5 method: the lower of FCF yield and trailing earnings yield, Bear / Base / Bull growth weighted 30% / 50% / 20%, 2.5% terminal growth.
  • Required return = the sector rate plus 2 points for small-company risk (data/required_returns.json).
  • Margin of safety 35% (40% for Energy and Basic Materials), 5 points more than the Top 5.
  • The same sanity filters as the Top 5 (loss-making, FCF distorted or unusually high, one-off growth, high debt, weak interest cover). Anything that looks more than 2.5× undervalued is marked Check first.

5. Quick answers

  • Every stock that passed the gate gets the Growth Screen's five quick questions: revenue growing, profits healthy, cash flow strong, debt manageable, financial position improving. Required return for the cost-of-capital test = sector rate + 2 points.
  • Passes all 5 checks keeps only stocks answering yes to all five, and Passes at least 4 checks keeps stocks answering yes to four or five (a "partly" or "no" counts as a miss). Both work in whichever view is chosen (for example All that passed). Hover the Checks badge for the answers. Banks can't pass, since the cash-flow and debt questions don't apply to them.
  • Sector: Insurance keeps insurers (any industry with "Insurance" in its name, brokers included, as on the Growth Screen). The cash-flow and debt questions don't apply to insurers (N/A), so with this filter on, as in the Growth Screen's Insurance top 5, N/A counts as passed for the two Checks filters and the Checks badge. Outside the Insurance filter, insurers are counted the strict way like every other stock.

Once a month

  • Runs on the 2nd of each month (after the main screen), when a new IWM file is uploaded, or by hand (Actions → Russell 2000 screen). A stock gets a full model on the Fair Value tab only when it is added by hand.
