How the Top 5 are chosen

1. Start with this month's Top 50 + 5 resilient compounders

  • The 50 highest Growth Quality Scores on the Growth Screen tab, plus the 5 resilient compounders added after them (from November 2026).

2. Remove stocks whose numbers can't be trusted

  • Banks and insurers (free cash flow isn't meaningful). Fee-based financials such as V and MA are allowed.
  • No P/E, or loss-making.
  • FCF distorted: free cash flow under 25% of earnings (heavy capex).
  • FCF unusually high: over 1.5× earnings (often one-off timing, may not last).
  • Acquisition-like growth: sales up over 40% on a net margin under 5%, or sales more than doubled.
  • Unstable: sales growth swung by over 30 points, or free cash flow flipped sign, since the last real month.
  • Balance sheet: net debt over 4× EBITDA, or EBIT under 3× interest (from November 2026).

3. Quick fair value (Bear / Base / Bull, like the main model)

  • Cash yield = the lower of FCF yield and trailing earnings yield (actual results, not forecasts). Energy and Basic Materials take a 20% mid-cycle haircut.
  • Growth: recent sales growth (0 to 40%, cyclicals capped at 10%). Year-1 growth is 25% / 50% / 75% of it for Bear / Base / Bull (caps 8% / 15% / 25%), fading to 2% / 3% / 4% by year 10.
  • 10 years of cash discounted at the sector required return, plus a terminal value at 2.5% growth. Weights 30% / 50% / 20%.
  • Fair value per share = price at evaluation × (value ÷ market value). Buy below = fair value minus a 30% margin of safety (35% for cyclicals).

4. Pick

  • The 5 with the biggest discount to fair value, at most 2 from one sector. Anything that looks more than 2.5× undervalued goes to Check first instead: that is usually a one-off gain or a data error, not a bargain.

Once a month

  • Runs by itself on the 1st (the screener runs on GitHub first), when you upload a month's CSV, or from the button. A second run in the same month does nothing. Past months stay so you can see how earlier picks did.
